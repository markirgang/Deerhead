📖 Animatronic Deerhead Operating Manual
1. Wall Mounting: Secure the wooden shield plaque to a vertical wall stud or anchor.
2. Powering On: Connect the +12V DC power supply to the L298 motor driver and +5V UBEC to the Raspberry Pi.
3. AI Conversation: Click 'Start Mic VAD' in the Gemini Voice AI tab to speak hands-free. The deerhead mouth will open and close in real-time sync with AI speech.
4. Song Performances: Import MP3 song files into the Music tab to watch the deerhead sing and bow on beat drops!
