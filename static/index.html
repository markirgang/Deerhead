⚙️ L298 MOTOR DRIVER LOGIC TRUTH TABLE
• Mouth Open (Channel 1 Active): ENA = HIGH (100% PWM), IN1 = HIGH, IN2 = LOW → Lower jaw opens.
• Mouth Closed (Channel 1 Inactive): ENA = LOW, IN1 = LOW, IN2 = LOW → Jaw spring pulls mouth closed.
• Head Bow Forward (Channel 2 Active): ENB = HIGH (90% PWM), IN3 = HIGH, IN4 = LOW → Deerhead bows forward.
• Head Return Upright (Channel 2 Inactive): ENB = LOW, IN3 = LOW, IN4 = LOW → Neck returns upright to vertical wall position.
📖 Wall-Mounted Animatronic Deerhead Operating Manual & Assembly Guide
Complete step-by-step instructions for hardware assembly, wall mounting, power wiring, AI setup, and song performance features.
1. 🏰 MECHANICAL WALL MOUNTING & TROPHY SHIELD SETUP
1. Mounting Location: Choose a vertical wall surface with a wall stud or heavy-duty wall anchor capable of supporting 5-8 lbs.
2. Shield Plaque: Secure the dark oak wooden trophy shield plaque vertically against the wall.
3. Neck Bowing Joint: Ensure the neck bowing pivot hinge is aligned horizontally so the head bows straight forward.
4. Mouth Jaw Clearance: Check that the lower jaw moves freely up and down without rubbing against the snout coat.
2. ⚡ ELECTRICAL WIRING & POWER DISTRIBUTION
1. 12V Power Input: Connect the +12V DC power adapter to the L298 Motor Driver `VCC` (+) and `GND` (-) screw terminals.
2. 5V UBEC Converter: Connect the 5V 3A UBEC step-down regulator from the 12V rail to the Raspberry Pi 5V power input.
3. Common Ground (GND): CRITICAL SAFETY: Connect Raspberry Pi GND (Pin 6) to L298 GND and Power Supply (-) GND to establish a shared ground reference.
4. Motor Pins: Connect L298 OUT1/OUT2 to the Lower Jaw Mouth Motor (Channel 1), and OUT3/OUT4 to the Neck Bowing Pivot Motor (Channel 2).
3. 🎤 AUDIO SPEAKER & CAMERA VISION SETUP
1. Audio Speaker: Connect a USB speaker or 3.5mm AUX powered amplifier to the Raspberry Pi audio output for clear voice and song playback.
2. Camera Lens: Position a USB webcam or Pi CSI camera in the snout nose/eye lens area pointing forward towards the room.
4. 🤖 SOFTWARE OPERATION & GEMINI AI FEATURES
1. Starting Server: Open a terminal and run `python3 server.py --port 1490`. Access the Web UI at http://localhost:1490.
2. Gemini API Key: Set your key in the ⚙️ Settings Tab or side panel input.
3. Hands-Free AI Voice Chat: Click 🎙️ Start Mic VAD in the Gemini tab to speak hands-free. The lower jaw will open/close in real-time sync with AI vocal responses while bowing on greetings.
4. MP3 Song Singing & URL Downloads: In the 🎵 Music & Singing Tab, drop local MP3 files or paste a direct MP3 URL to download tracks directly into the playlist. Click 🎤 Play & Sing to watch the deerhead lipsync to vocals and bow to beat drops!
5. ⚠️ TROUBLESHOOTING & SAFETY RULES
• Mouth Doesn't Move: Verify L298 Channel 1 jumper ENA is removed or connected to Pi GPIO 17 (Pin 11). Check 12V power supply connection.
• No Audio Sync: Ensure your browser has permitted microphone and Web Audio API permissions.
• Exporting Code: Click Export Python Code in the header to download standalone Raspberry Pi hardware control scripts (deerhead_animatronic_control.py).
