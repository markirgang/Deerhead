3x L298N Drivers

Motor Channel Allocation & Driver Specification

L298N Controller #1 — Front Wheel Motors

FRONT WHEELS
  • OUT 1 / OUT 2: Front-Left (FL) Wheel DC Motor
  • OUT 3 / OUT 4: Front-Right (FR) Wheel DC Motor
  • Inputs: ENA/IN1/IN2 (GPIO 18,23,24) | ENB/IN3/IN4 (GPIO 19,25,8)
  • ⚡ Power: Connected to +7.4V/+12V Battery Rail

L298N Controller #2 — Rear Wheel Motors

REAR WHEELS
  • OUT 1 / OUT 2: Rear-Left (RL) Wheel DC Motor
  • OUT 3 / OUT 4: Rear-Right (RR) Wheel DC Motor
  • Inputs: ENA/IN1/IN2 (GPIO 12,16,20) | ENB/IN3/IN4 (GPIO 13,21,26)
  • ⚡ Power: Shared +7.4V Battery Rail

L298N Controller #3 — Head Mouth & Neck Motors

HEAD & NECK
  • OUT 1 / OUT 2: Mouth Opening Motor (Speech Audio Sync)
  • OUT 3 / OUT 4: Pulsating Neck Actuator (Volume VU Sync)
  • Inputs: ENA/IN1/IN2 (GPIO 17,27,22) | ENB/IN3/IN4 (GPIO 4,5,6)
  • 🗣️ Voice Sync: Automatic motor activation during AI speech

4WD Rover & Robotic Head Operating Manual & User Guide

Complete Setup, Realtime Driving, Gemini AI Voice & Vision, Audio Lip-Sync, and Maintenance Instructions

📋 Rev 2.4 🤖 4WD Rover + Head 🎙️ Gemini AI Voice 📡 Autonomous Avoidance
Step 1

⚡ Quick Start & Powering On

  • 1. Power Supply Connection: Connect a 2S (7.4V) or 3S (11.1V) LiPo battery to the high-current power terminal bus. Ensure the 5V 10A UBEC buck regulator powers the Raspberry Pi logic.
  • 2. Start Web Controller: Launch the Python server terminal command:
    python3 server.py
  • 3. Open Workbench: Navigate to http://localhost:9090 in your browser (or use IP http://<your-pi-ip>:9090).
  • 4. Connection Status: Ensure the status badge at top right displays ONLINE.
Step 2

🏎️ Realtime Driving & Keyboard Shortcuts

  • • Forward / Reverse: Press W or ↑ to drive forward; press S or ↓ to reverse.
  • • Turning & Spinning: Press A / D to steer left/right; press Q / E for 360° spin turns in place.
  • • Emergency Stop: Press Spacebar or click ■ Stop button to instantly kill wheel motors.
  • • 4-Wheel Speed Sliders: Individually adjust PWM speed (0-100%) and direction for Front-Left, Front-Right, Rear-Left, and Rear-Right wheels in the left panel.
Step 3

🤖 Gemini AI Multimodal Agent & Camera Vision

  • 1. API Key Setup: Enter your Google Gemini API key in the AI panel or under ⚙️ Settings & HW.
  • 2. Activate Microphone: Click 🎙️ Activate Mic to start live hands-free speech recognition with visual VU sound indicator.
  • 3. Live Scene Description: Click 👁️ Describe Scene to capture eMeet Pixy webcam footage and have Gemini describe the environment out loud!
  • 4. AI Voice & Regional Accents: Choose between male and female neural voices across US, UK, Australian, Canadian, Indian, and South African accents in Settings.
Step 4

🗣️ Robotic Head Mouth & Neck Actuation

  • • Speech Lip-Sync: L298N Driver #3 Channel 1 automatically energizes the black robotic mouth motor in sync with AI speech synthesis and MP3 audio playback.
  • • Pulsating Neck Motion: L298N Driver #3 Channel 2 pulses the neck mechanism proportionally to the microphone and speaker volume VU meter.
  • • Manual Testing: Use the L298 Motor State panel in the right sidebar to manually trigger mouth opening or neck pulsation for hardware testing.
Step 5

🎵 MP3 Music Player & Rover Beat Dance

  • 1. Upload Music Files: Click 📁 Import MP3 to static/audio/ Folder to load custom audio tracks into the rover's local storage.
  • 2. Select Dance Routines: Choose from preset routines including Macarena, Techno Beat Shuffle, K-Pop Groove, and Disco Boogie.
  • 3. Synchronized Drive: Click ▶️ Play & Dance to run wheel dance choreography, mouth speech lip-sync, and music simultaneously!
Step 6

📡 Autonomous Avoidance & Safety Maintenance

  • • Ultrasonic Array: 4x HC-SR04 sensors continuously sample 360° obstacle distances (Front, Rear, Left, Right).
  • • Auto Steering: Toggle Enable Ultrasonic Obstacle Avoidance in Settings. When enabled, the rover automatically steers away if front distance drops below 35 cm.
  • • Battery Protection: Always disconnect the LiPo battery when not in use. Ensure 1kΩ / 2kΩ resistor dividers step down 5V Echo lines to 3.3V Pi GPIO levels.
